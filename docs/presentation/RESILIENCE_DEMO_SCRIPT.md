## Q&A Preparation - Resilience Questions

### "What happens if the system goes down completely?"
**Answer:**
"We've designed multiple layers of protection. First, automatic failover to backup systems. Second, offline mode for agents. Third, manual override procedures. In 18 months of testing, we've never had a complete system failure affect operations. But even if it happened, staff are trained on emergency procedures that maintain safety and compliance."

### "How do you ensure data isn't lost during connectivity issues?"
**Answer:**
"Every agent device has local storage that automatically caches critical data. When connectivity is restored, our synchronization engine merges all changes with conflict detection. We use timestamp-based resolution and maintain complete audit trails. In our testing, we've achieved 100% data recovery even in extended offline scenarios."

### "What about cybersecurity and system attacks?"
**Answer:**
"Security is built into every layer. Encrypted data transmission, secure authentication, and isolated system architecture. Even if one component is compromised, the system continues operating with reduced functionality rather than complete failure. We follow aviation industry cybersecurity standards and conduct regular security audits."

### "How long does it take to recover from a major system failure?"
**Answer:**
"Our recovery time objective is 15 minutes for complete system restoration. Most issues are resolved automatically within seconds through failover. For the rare major incident, we have hot standby systems that can be activated immediately. During recovery, manual procedures ensure operations continue without flight delays."

### "Do agents need special training for emergency procedures?"
**Answer:**
"Minimal additional training required. Since our system builds on existing weight & balance procedures, agents already know the fundamentals. We add just 2 hours of emergency procedure training covering offline mode and manual override. The interface is designed to be intuitive even under stress."
